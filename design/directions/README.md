# Identity directions (exploration record)

*Reference. The three directions explored for the identity (WP-04),
kept as a record. **These are explorations, not for use:** do not ship,
copy or export anything from this folder. The final files and rules are
in the [style guide](../README.md); why Direction 1 was chosen and what
was taken from the others is in
[About the visual identity](../../docs/explanation/about-the-visual-identity.md).*

| Folder | Direction | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [`1/`](1/notes.md) | The twin temple | Chosen; refined into `design/logo/` |
| [`2/`](2/notes.md) | The step-fret band | Its band became the secondary pattern (`design/patterns/`) |
| [`3/`](3/notes.md) | Three treasures in a row | Idea left for store art (WP-38) |

Each folder has `notes.md`, `palette.md`, `type.md` and the `build.py`
that drew it. Their colours, type choices and contrast figures are
superseded by the style guide.

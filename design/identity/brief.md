# Identity brief

*The one-page brief the Montezuma's visual identity (WP-04) answers. Why
the chosen direction: [About the visual identity](../../docs/explanation/about-the-visual-identity.md).
The resulting rules: [Style guide](../README.md).*

## Product and audience

Montezuma's is a free, open-source 3D match-3 game for Windows, macOS,
Linux, Android, iOS and the web, remaking the gameplay of *The Treasures
of Montezuma* (Visual Shape and Alawar, 2006). It is set in Tenochtitlan
in the reign of Moctezuma II (1502 to 1520), following the sourced
[Mexica art reference](../../docs/reference/history-mexica-art.md).

Players are casual and puzzle players of every age, many of whom
remember the original; some will meet Mexica art here for the first time.
They should feel that the game is warm, bright and inviting, and that it
takes the culture it borrows from seriously.

## Personality

Is: **bright**, **crafted**, **grounded** (in sources), **welcoming**,
**confident**.

Is not: **jungle-adventure** (Hollywood temples, vines, treasure-hunter
pulp), **grim** (skulls, blood, sacrifice), **pastiche** (neo-Aztec
revival, Sun Stone posters, faux-glyph lettering).

Print and merchandise are out of scope.

## Constraints

- All art our own, GPL-2.0-or-later; third-party fonts only under OFL
  (project profile). Nothing from the original game's files.
- Sources win over the remembered original. From the Mexica art
  reference: flat colour inside a black outline for graphics; five stone
  pigment families plus the richer painted-book palette for UI; step-fret
  and colour bands for frames; no pseudo-glyphs, no day signs, no deity
  faces, no Sun Stone likeness, no eagle on a nopal, no skulls, no jungle.
- Keep the seven piece colours readable as today's HUD swatches
  (`design/tokens/temple-board.json`, generated by the technical artist).
- Latin script, with accents for Spanish, French, Portuguese and German
  and Nahuatl names in their usual Latin spelling (for example
  *Motecuhzoma*, *Tenochtitlan*, *xicalcoliuhqui*).
- Must work light and dark: the site follows the system scheme, the game
  UI is dark over the 3D board.
- Token names already read by `site/src/assets/tokens.css` and
  `game/ui/theme/placeholder.tokens.json` stay, so the swap is one file
  each.

## Approver and done-when

The coordinator (project manager) accepts the package; the code reviewer
checks it. Done when the work-package row's criteria hold: a logo system,
palette, type, style guide and tokens, with contrast and small-size
checks passing and every font licence recorded.

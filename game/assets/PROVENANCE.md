  - game/assets/materials/shaders/SH_Fire*.gdshader, SH_Ember*.gdshader, SH_HeatHaze.gdshader (hand-written)

notes: >
  The work package asked for a Blender fluid simulation. Blender's solver (Mantaflow) could

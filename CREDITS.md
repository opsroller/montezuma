# Credits

Montezuma's is a new game that plays like *The Treasures of Montezuma*.
Its code, art, sound and text are the original work of the people below,
licensed GPL-2.0-or-later. The in-game credits screen and the website
both read this file. Add yourself under the right heading; don't rewrite
other people's lines.

## Inspiration

- *The Treasures of Montezuma* (PC 2006, iOS 2009), the match-3 game developed by
  Visual Shape and published by Alawar Entertainment, that Montezuma's is
  modelled on. None of its files are used.

# Rules sources

The evidence behind the rules spec. Ids `[S*]` are cited by the rules
pages. Each entry gives what the source shows, summarised in our own
words; nothing is copied from the game or from the sources. Read on
2026-10-18.

Reference version: *The Treasures of Montezuma* as documented for the PC
release (2006) and its ports; where the 2009 iOS listing differs, the
difference is in [About the rules](../explanation/about-the-rules.md).

| Id | Source | Kind | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | [StrategyWiki, Stages](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/The_Treasures_of_Montezuma/Stages) | walkthrough (level 4) | 5 stages of 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 levels; per-level unlocks and tiers of the six power-ups, seven totems, timer, gems on board and Score Frenzy; golden stars and star sand spent on upgrades; the 8 bonus levels and where they fall |
| S2 | [StrategyWiki, main page](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/The_Treasures_of_Montezuma) | walkthrough (level 4) | 7 token colours, 6 power-ups, 4 bonus level types; totems fire on two or more consecutive matches of their colour; Score Frenzy bar fills from matched tokens and doubles points |
| S3 | [MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/game/54770) | database description (level 4) | goal is a gem count before the timer ends, rising 5 per level up to 200; 41 levels in 5 chapters; two shuffles per level; hints bought with points; Hard mode unlocked after Adventure, with higher goals |
| S4 | [Pocket Gamer review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/treasures-of-montezuma/review) | review (level 5) | gems inside some tokens, released when matched; fixed time limit while the gem goal rises; no moves left restarts the grid with a small time penalty; 4 and 5 matches give special tokens; totems on two consecutive same-colour matches |
| S5 | [All About Windows Phone review](https://allaboutwindowsphone.com/reviews/item/15198_Treasures_of_Montezuma.php) | review (level 5) | gems sit on stones and are collected when matched; totem fires after two consecutive matches of its colour (green collects every gem); special items sit on stones and trigger like gems; stars bought between levels |
| S6 | [TouchArcade listing](https://toucharcade.com/games/the-treasures-of-montezuma) | store listing (level 3) | iOS (26 Mar 2009): 53 levels, 5 episodes, 2 modes, 4 bonus games with 8 bonus levels, 8 bonuses, 6 totems, 9 trophies |
| S7 | [App Store, HD Lite](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/id390141724) | store listing (level 3) | iPad: full game 41 levels; 6 bonuses, 6 totems, 9 trophies; later version added upgrades |
| S8 | [Cheatbook save-file notes](https://www.cheatbook.de/files/treasuresofmontezuma.htm) | player notes (level 5) | names of the stored items, which match S1 (seven coloured totems with their powers, six power-ups, Frenzy and gem amount), with 3 or 4 tiers each |

Levels follow the evidence ladder of the design-reconstruction method:
3 = publisher or store text, 4 = walkthrough or database that shows its
evidence, 5 = review or player notes. A rule is confirmed with two
independent sources; otherwise it is **[chosen]**.

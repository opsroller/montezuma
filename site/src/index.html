A free, open-source 3D match-3 game. Beat the intro level to get the full game.

Skip the level and get the game

Swap two neighbouring gems by clicking or tapping one and then the other, or by swiping. Escape pauses. The game keeps the keyboard while it has focus; press Shift+Escape to get back to the page.
